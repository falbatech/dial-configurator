1. Getting started

  1. Connect DIAL FT to your computer (macOS, Windows or Linux) with the USB-C cable. No drivers are needed.
  2. After the FalbaTech logo a short tutorial appears: turn, click, hold, swipe and tap, one after another.
  3. Open Settings → Host OS and choose your system (macOS or Windows). It decides whether shortcuts use Cmd or Ctrl.
  4. Pick an app on the HOME screen by turning the dial and open it with a click or a tap.
Every profile works right away, with no software on your computer. To the computer DIAL FT is a USB keyboard and mouse. The optional DIAL FT Helper adds Spotify cover art, automatic profile switching and the clock.
HOME screen, Color theme.

The screen dims after 45 s of inactivity (or shows the clock). The first turn, click or touch only wakes it.

Spotify

Tracks, volume and play/pause use media keys and work even with Spotify in the background. SEEK and LIKE need the Spotify window in front. With DIAL FT Helper the screen shows cover art, title, artist and track progress.

Meetings

Click to choose the app: ZOOM, TEAMS or MEET (Google Meet in a browser). Tap mutes the microphone - the RGB ring then pulses red. The CAM button toggles the camera. DIAL keeps track of the state itself; if you mute with the mouse, tap twice to get back in sync.

5. DaVinci Resolve: COLOR

The COLOR mode in the DaVinci profile controls the real controls on the Color page - no software needed, also in the free DaVinci Resolve.

  1. In the DaVinci profile click until HOVER + TURN appears.
  2. Point the mouse at a value, e.g. Temp, Tint, Contrast, Saturation or the ribbed bar under the Lift / Gamma / Gain / Offset wheel.
  3. Turn the dial. A tap changes the step: FINE / NORMAL / COARSE. The screen rim gives the smallest steps.
Turning works like dragging with the mouse. Keep the pointer over a value - over another item (e.g. a node) it would move it.

6. Smart Touch Ring and Smart Scroll

Smart Touch Ring: slide your finger around the screen edge. It works like a second, finer dial. Settings per profile: Settings → Touch ring (or swipe left from the RGB ring screen).

Touch modeSMOOTH or STEP
SensitivityLOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
Inertiamomentum after a flick: OFF / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
DirectionNORMAL / REVERSE
Adaptivefaster movement = bigger changes
Touch ringon / off for this profile

Smart Scroll: scrolling with acceleration (SMART) or constant (PRECISION), momentum and vertical / horizontal axis (click). Reversing the direction stops the momentum immediately; so does touching the screen.

7. Pomodoro, breaks and clock

Pomodoro: turn to set the focus time (when stopped), click or tap to start / pause, RESET to start over. Set the break in Settings → Pomo break. When a period ends the screen blinks and the ring flashes white. The timer keeps running while you use other profiles.

Statistics: sessions today and in the last 7 days (when DIAL knows the time - see below).

Stand-up reminder: after 50 minutes of work without a 5-minute break DIAL shows “TIME TO STAND UP”. Any input dismisses it. Turn it off in Settings → Stand-up.

Clock face: when idle DIAL shows the time, date, seconds ring, Pomodoro and the song title. DIAL has no battery clock - the time is set by the configurator (when connected) or by DIAL FT Helper (permanently). Without it the screen simply dims.

10. Configurator and custom profiles

The configurator is the page falbatech.click/dial-ft. It works in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer - nothing to install. Everything is saved in DIAL, so it then works on any computer.

  1. Plug in DIAL FT and click Connect DIAL FT. Choose DIAL FT in the window.
  2. General: theme, system, brightness, RGB ring, Pomodoro, auto profile, clock, reminder.
  3. Home screen: untick apps you do not use.
  4. Your own profiles: up to 12 profiles for any program, from templates or from scratch.
  5. Click Save to DIAL FT.

Custom profile

  • Name, icon and color (shown in the Color theme). A template (“Start from template”) fills the profile with ready shortcuts, e.g. for a browser, DaVinci, Premiere, Photoshop, Lightroom, Figma or VS Code.
  • Up to 4 modes. Every mode has 4 actions: turn left, turn right, tap and dial click (empty click = next mode).
  • Every action can be a macro - up to 6 steps in a row: keyboard shortcut (click the field and press it), media key, mouse wheel, pause, type text, next / previous mode.
  • Two texts per profile (up to 32 characters, ASCII) for the “Type text” step, e.g. a signature or an e-mail address.
  • Program (optional): app identifier for auto profile, e.g. com.apple.Safari (macOS) or chrome.exe (Windows).
  • Export / Import profiles: save all profiles to a file and load them back - a backup or a way to share profiles.
“Ctrl/Cmd” in shortcuts is sent as Cmd on Mac and Ctrl on Windows - following the Host OS setting. One profile works on both systems.

11. Firmware update

Install updates on the same page (falbatech.click/dial-ft), in the Firmware update section. Settings and custom profiles are kept.

  1. Click 1. Prepare and choose DIAL FT. The screen goes dark - DIAL is in update mode.
  2. Click 2. Install update and choose the port “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”.
  3. When it reports success, unplug and plug in DIAL FT.

If DIAL does not enter update mode: unplug the cable, hold the BOOT button on the back of the board, plug the cable in, release the button and start from step 2.

12. DIAL FT Helper (optional, macOS)

A small background program. Download it from the configurator. It adds:

  • Spotify cover art, title, artist and progress on the DIAL screen,
  • automatic profile switching when you change apps (DaVinci, Premiere, Final Cut, Photoshop, Lightroom, Pixelmator, Spotify, Zoom, Teams, Figma, Blender, Fusion 360, KiCad and your profiles),
  • the time for the clock face and Pomodoro statistics.

Install: unzip the archive and run install_mac.sh in Terminal. The helper starts automatically at login. On the first song macOS asks for permission to control Spotify - click Allow. Uninstall: install_mac.sh --uninstall.

Auto profile does not switch while you are in Settings or Pomodoro. Turn it off in Settings → Auto profile.

13. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
Shortcuts do not work on Mac (or Windows)Set Settings → Host OS to match your computer.
Scrolling goes the wrong waySettings → Scroll dir or a tap in the Smart Scroll profile.
No clock when idleConnect the configurator or install DIAL FT Helper (DIAL has no battery clock).
COLOR moves something elseKeep the pointer over a value in Resolve, not over a node or clip.
No Spotify cover artRun DIAL FT Helper and allow it to control Spotify (System Settings → Privacy → Automation).
The configurator cannot see DIALUse Chrome or Edge, close other programs using the DIAL port, reconnect the cable.
DIAL does not respondUnplug and plug in the USB cable. Settings are not lost.
